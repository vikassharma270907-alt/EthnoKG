Finally, traditional knowledge mining demands the highest ethical standards. Indigenous intellectual property and empirical discoveries have historically been vulnerable to biopiracy and uncredited exploitation. EthnoKG strictly enforces the principles of the Nagoya Protocol on Access and Benefit Sharing. Every traditional claim explicitly attributes the originating cultural community and primary field ethnographer. Furthermore, to prevent the illegal or unsustainable wild-harvesting of endangered medicinal flora, high-resolution geospatial GPS coordinates are deliberately masked for species listed under IUCN and CITES protection. And as an inviolable scientific guardrail, EthnoKG maintains a strict non-diagnostic research scope: traditional indications are preserved as empirical cultural observations, never as substitute medical advice. In conclusion, EthnoKG bridges ancient empirical heritage with cutting-edge computational intelligence, transforming fragmented traditional medicine into a structured, traceable, and ethically governed foundation for modern natural product discovery.
