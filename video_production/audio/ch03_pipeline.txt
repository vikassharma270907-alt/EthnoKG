At the intellectual heart of EthnoKG lies our proprietary 12-stage AI literature mining pipeline. Instead of relying on fragile prompt engineering or raw black-box language models, we enforce a strict, twelve-step deterministic extraction workflow. In Stage 1, raw PDF documents and research text undergo Unicode normalization, ligature fixing, and hyphenation de-breaking. Stage 2 performs semantic paragraph segmentation, classifying sections into abstracts, ethnobotanical narratives, experimental bioassays, and results. Stage 3 executes domain-adapted scientific named entity recognition to identify candidate botanical taxa, ailments, and phytochemicals. Stage 4 normalizes botanical species against Kew Medicinal Plant Names Services and World Flora Online, reconciling historical vernacular synonyms without false merges. Stage 5 maps extracted medical conditions to standardized MeSH and ICD-11 terminology. Stage 6 cross-references phytochemical compounds against PubChem and ChEMBL identifiers. Stage 7 extracts the specific plant anatomical organ, whether leaves, roots, bark, or rhizomes, alongside traditional preparations, such as water decoctions, alcoholic macerations, or powders. Stage 8 formulates formal Subject-Predicate-Object triples. Stage 9 categorizes the claim into our six-tier scientific evidence hierarchy. Stage 10 computes calibrated confidence scores based on lexical certainty and taxonomic verification. Stage 11 permanently binds the claim to its verbatim source excerpt, sentence anchor, author, and DOI. Finally, Stage 12 stages the verified extraction for curator review and live knowledge graph ingestion.
