EthnoKG provides three specialized environments for advanced research workflows. First, the Evidence and Provenance Explorer allows scientists to filter claims across our six rigorous evidence tiers, distinguishing between traditional folkloric reports, ethnobotanical surveys, in-vitro bioassays, in-vivo animal studies, and randomized clinical trials. Second, the Grounded AI Research Assistant offers an intelligent conversational interface. Unlike generalized chat bots that suffer from hallucinations, our assistant is strictly grounded in the indexed SQLite database. When asking about traditional remedies for fever or anti-inflammatory properties of Zingiber officinale, the assistant responds with precise, evidence-backed syntheses, cites exact primary publications, and conspicuously displays our non-diagnostic medical disclaimer. Third, the Human Verification Workspace provides a curator queue where ethnopharmacologists and botanists can review staged AI extractions. Curators can inspect the candidate extraction side-by-side with the original text, modify plant names or ailment mappings, and approve or reject claims with a permanent cryptographic audit log.
