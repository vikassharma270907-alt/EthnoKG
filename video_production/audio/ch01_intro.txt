Welcome to this comprehensive technical presentation of EthnoKG: Turning Traditional Knowledge into Traceable Scientific Evidence, built for EthnoHack 2026. For millennia, human civilizations and indigenous communities across the globe have cultivated profound, empirical pharmacopeias — from Ayurveda in the Indian subcontinent and Traditional Chinese Medicine, to African folk medicine and Amazonian ethnobotany. Yet today, this irreplaceable body of knowledge remains fundamentally siloed, trapped in narrative historical texts, unstructured field surveys, and isolated research papers. Modern drug discovery pipelines face four critical bottlenecks: first, ethnobotanical claims are deeply buried within qualitative prose; second, taxonomic synonymy causes widespread botanical misidentification and erroneous species merges; third, evidence levels are conflated — folkloric anecdotes are often flattened alongside rodent models and clinical trials; and fourth, citations are completely severed from primary voucher specimens and verbatim source passages. EthnoKG solves this dilemma through an end-to-end, reproducible AI literature mining pipeline and an interactive evidence-linked Knowledge Graph, ensuring that every single entity, bioactivity relationship, and preparation method is cryptographically anchored to its original verbatim scientific literature.
