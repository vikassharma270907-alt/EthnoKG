Now, let us step into the live running web application to demonstrate EthnoKG in action. We begin on the Landing Page, which introduces the scientific mission, core research tracks, and live platform telemetry. Navigating to the Research Discovery Dashboard, the interface immediately queries our live SQLite database to render real-time quantitative metrics. Here, domain researchers can observe our verified corpus: twelve authoritative botanical species, twenty-five curated bioactivity relationships, twelve linked phytochemical compounds, and eighteen indexed peer-reviewed scientific publications, maintaining an extraordinary average extraction confidence of ninety-five point four percent. Below the hero metrics, an interactive evidence distribution chart visualizes the breakdown across scientific tiers, highlighting in-vitro bioassays, in-vivo rodent models, clinical human trials, and traditional use reports. A second radar chart maps cultural traditions, displaying representation across Ayurveda, Traditional Chinese Medicine, African Traditional Medicine, Amazonian Ethnobotany, and European Folk Medicine. The real-time ingestion feed on the right displays newly indexed papers, showing author credentials, character counts, and relationship yields.
